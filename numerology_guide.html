Notes on the source data

The calculator follows the workbook's rules. Where its worked examples or tables disagree with those rules, the rule is used and the difference is listed here.

  • Destiny and Personality examples. In the “VIDYA SINGH” example, H is counted as 9 in one row (it is 8 elsewhere), and the vowel A is counted in the consonant row. The printed answers (Destiny 11, Personality 3) therefore differ from the rules applied correctly (Destiny 1, Personality 11).
  • Fourth pinnacle example. 3 + 1982 is shown as 4; applying the rule gives 5.
  • Pinnacle ages. The example text says “35 to 43” for the second pinnacle; the table says 36–44. The table is used.
  • Intensified numbers. One note says to take all tied numbers, another says only the top two. All tied numbers are shown.
  • Y and W as vowels. The note says “if the preceding letter is a vowel”, but the VIDYA example counts Y as a vowel because an A follows it. A neighbouring vowel on either side is used.
  • Chaldean table. S is listed under 5 and X under 3, which differs from many published tables. It is used as written.
  • Combination matrix. Symbols are not explained, and some (6 & 9, 2 & 3) differ from the named lists. Both are shown.
  • Life-period table. Some entries overlap or contain typos (for example “51`”). They are shown as printed.
