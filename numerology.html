The foundation

Moolank and Bhagyank

Moolank (Mulank) — Birth Number

Your day of birth reduced to one digit. Born on the 25th: 2 + 5 = 7.

Bhagyank — Life Path Number

Every digit of your full date of birth added, then reduced. 16/07/2002: 1+6+0+7+2+0+0+2 = 18 → 1+8 = 9.
