How to read it

Eight directions, one score each

For every room the workbook gives a rating such as Highly Auspicious, Auspicious, Can Be Better or Inauspicious, plus a score from 1 to 10, for each of the eight directions: North, North-East, East, South-East, South, South-West, West and North-West.

The centre of the home, the Brahmasthan, is named in the workbook but has no rating, so it is shown as not rated.

The overall Vastu Meter is the average of the scores for the rooms you enter. It is a simple summary added by this website, and is not a rule from the workbook.
