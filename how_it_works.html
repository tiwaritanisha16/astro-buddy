How it works

Two services, each with its own simple steps.

Numerology

Your numbers

  1. Enter your details

    Name and date of birth. Time and place of birth, and photos, are optional.

  2. Numbers are calculated

    Moolank is your birth day reduced to one digit. Bhagyank adds every digit of your date of birth. The calculator adds the name-based and cycle numbers.

  3. Read the free preview

    Your numbers with their working, a personality overview, strengths and challenges.

  4. Open the complete report

    Full guidance for the topics you chose, reflection prompts and a printable layout. {{ 'Payment is a demo on this installation.' if demo_payment else '' }}

Vastu

Your rooms

  1. Pick your rooms

    In the Vastu Meter, choose the direction each room of your home sits in.

  2. Ratings are looked up

    Each room and direction has a rating and a score from 1 to 10 in the Vastu workbook.

  3. See your meter

    You get every rating, the best direction for each room, and the average score.

  4. Browse the guide

    The Room-by-Room Guide shows all eight directions for any room.

About optional photos

In the Personal Reading you may add a face and/or a palm photo. Both are optional. If you add one and give your consent, that image is sent to Google Gemini to write a speculative, traditional-style reflection. The calculators and the Vastu tools never use photos. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
